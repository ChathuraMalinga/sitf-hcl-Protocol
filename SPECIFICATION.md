# SITF-HCL Protocol: High-Level Architecture & IP Disclosure
**Founder & Primary Inventor:** Chathura Malinga  
**Commercial Entity:** Fairdigit  
**Document Type:** Technical Framework & Intellectual Property Claim  

## 1. Executive Summary
The SITF-HCL (Social Impact Trust Framework - Human Capital Ledger) Protocol is a proprietary methodology for the conversion of professional performance metrics into a decentralized, privacy-preserved trust score. This document outlines the functional requirements and architectural claims of the protocol without disclosing protected algorithmic variables.

## 2. IP Claims & Inventive Steps
The founder, Chathura Malinga, claims the following as proprietary intellectual property:

### A. The Multilateral Attestation Process (MAP)
A unique three-tier validation system where professional milestones are verified by an external issuer, hashed for privacy, and committed to a human capital ledger.

### B. The Privacy-Preserving Merit Tokenization
The specific process of using cryptographic hashing (SHA-256) combined with a **Proprietary Security Salt** to ensure that professional merit is verifiable by third parties without exposing the underlying Personal Identifiable Information (PII).

### C. The Predictive Trust Index ($T_i$)
A proprietary multi-weighted scoring system that analyzes a worker's ledger history to predict organizational retention and institutional stability. 
*(Note: The exact weights and algebraic constants are classified as trade secrets.)*

## 3. Operational Framework
The protocol operates through a decentralized "Voucher" system.
* **Input Layer:** Normalized career milestones (Tenure, Certifications, Impact Events).
* **Processing Layer:** Application of the proprietary SITF weighting engine.
* **Output Layer:** A singular, auditable 'Trust Index' score stored as a cryptographically signed entry.
